Methodology, coverage and limitations

Independent evaluation is evidence presence, not proof of improvement or future coverage.

Reapplication is applied-but-unvalidated until this application receives a qualifying independent assessment.

Conformal coverage is marginal under exchangeable scores and independent fixed upstream choices.

Cluster inference does not establish conformal validity. Selective labels limit population claims.

Fixed-artifact intervals exclude training, fitting and selection variability.

Observed separation checks apply only to supplied identities; external provenance remains declared.

Sampled resource controls can overshoot and are not capacity guarantees.

Metrics describe supplied outcomes and declared populations; ground truth and representativeness are not independently established.

Calibration bins and high-confidence settings are investigation definitions, not universal safety thresholds.

Policy replay is retrospective; different actions may change outcomes or their observation.

Statistical intervals, when enabled, are assumption-qualified; the recorded adjustment distinguishes pointwise and family confidence.

Sampled resource safeguards are not hard quotas or capacity guarantees.

No composite score, certification or production approval is defined.

Frozen role populations; common selection inputs and metric-specific paired evaluation. No silent sampling or population renegotiation.

Analytical bounds, retained-detail omissions, presentation omissions and privacy withholding are distinct. An unavailable metric is not zero. The protected local workbench contains exact retained evidence where supplied and eligible.

Methodology, coverage and limitations
Policy decisions are hypothetical offline replay; no deployment approval or causal business-value guarantee.
Observed-outcome, model-implied, operational and missing-label bounds are different estimands.
Conditional selective risk, full-opportunity behavior and deferral workload have different denominators.
Independent support conditions on frozen policies and declared sampling, observation, bounds and exposure assumptions.
Historical stability does not establish future effectiveness. Conformal marginal coverage is not conditional policy coverage.
Sampled resource controls can overshoot; admission defaults are not capacity guarantees.
Metrics describe supplied outcomes and declared populations; ground truth and representativeness are not independently established.
Calibration bins and high-confidence settings are investigation definitions, not universal safety thresholds.
Policy replay is retrospective; different actions may change outcomes or their observation.
Statistical intervals, when enabled, are assumption-qualified; the recorded adjustment distinguishes pointwise and family confidence.
Sampled resource safeguards are not hard quotas or capacity guarantees.
No composite score, certification or production approval is defined.
Analytical bounds, retained-detail omissions, presentation omissions and privacy withholding are distinct. An unavailable metric is not zero.
The protected local workbench contains exact retained evidence where supplied and eligible.

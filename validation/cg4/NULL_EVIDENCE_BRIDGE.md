# Current-selector bridge for retained finite-confirmation null evidence

This bridge implements the founder-approved closure revision. It does not turn
old-selector simulations into new-selector observations, qualify an approximate
procedure, or supply missing discovery-recovery evidence.

Let G contain D/S data, independent fitting randomness, declared feature/
representation transformations and the complete frozen family. The revised
`severity_material_excess_support_frontier_v2` selector uses S effects, conditional
support, complexity, overlap and uncovered-S contributions. Its membership inputs
are frozen D-fit gates; it neither consumes H outcomes nor refits on H. The new
material-excess/support anchor changes the choice of gates inside G.

For each retained finite procedure, conditional on G and the true declared sampling
and observation design, its elementary intervals have error at most a_hj. The
frozen family allocates sum(a_hj) <= alpha. Interval composition is conservative
under joint coverage, so

    P(any false practical confirmation | G) <= sum(a_hj) <= alpha.

Bonferroni does not require disjoint or independent hypotheses/components. A
random family size chosen on S is allowed when the *whole actual family* is frozen
before H; inadmissible/unavailable claims remain in that family. Integrating over
G preserves the bound. Eligibility denominators, membership-availability scope,
practical thresholds and transformations cannot be revised using H. Empty or
unsupported arms refuse rather than changing the estimand. Observed-label claims
remain observed-label claims. Entity-arm inference additionally requires its
recorded IID-complete-entity/one-potential-record-per-arm design.

The old 2,000 reduced and 200 default null runs, plus partial-null and frozen-kernel
simulations, retain their exact original profiles/counts. They exercise unchanged
finite interval arithmetic, overlap, allocation and refusal behavior. Their old
empirical error rates are not advertised as current-selector measurements.

The bridge additionally depends on current engineering invariants, checked through
the public workflow and focused tests:

- Changing H labels leaves D/S metrics, fitted artifacts, shortlist and frozen
  hypothesis definitions/family unchanged; source snapshot identities may change.
- The selected family is immutable; subsequent metric/observation-policy changes
  refuse. Every interval uses the full family allocation, including refused claims.
- H access follows inspection and reservation; reuse/unknown independence,
  unsupported dependence and deleted/restore-incomplete history cannot become fresh
  evidence through a new selection, source revision or workspace fork.
- The finite numerical kernels and authoritative reducers are unchanged from their
  retained reference/simulation evidence. Current qualification restrictions only
  remove unsupported formal approximate routes.

These are conditional scientific guarantees, not system proof of external
independence, completeness, true bounds, absence of selective labels or unobserved
common shocks. Passing local checks cannot establish those population assumptions.
Arbitrary repeated/adaptive investigations have no workspace-wide error guarantee.
Predeclared restatements retain their original family and allocated study schedule.

Therefore the frozen finite-procedure evidence can be reused with focused current
selection/freezing/admission tests. A new 2,000/200 simulation sweep is not needed
for this approved closure claim. Stronger current-profile empirical error-rate
claims, approximate methods, or changes to H separation/allocation/kernels would
require their own evidence and are not supplied by this bridge.

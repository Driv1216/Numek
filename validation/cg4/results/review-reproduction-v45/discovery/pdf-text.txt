Scope and limitations
Unavailable membership remains visible against the planned population. Conditional results cannot be read as full-population findings.
Observed labels do not establish unobserved outcomes. Local overlap checks do not verify external independence; missing exposure history
never proves freshness. Error control is study scoped, with no automatic guarantee across overlapping workspace studies.
Finite-sample procedures require their declared independence and observation assumptions. Approximate intervals require applicability
assessment against the recorded method-qualification evidence. Temporal common shocks, arbitrary adaptive reuse, and CG3 moving-block
bootstrap are not admitted CG4 confirmation routes. Historical restatements preserve original evidence and are descriptive unless an
inferential schedule was frozen in advance.
Methodology, coverage and limitations

Calibration bins and high-confidence settings are investigation definitions, not universal safety thresholds.
Policy replay is retrospective; different actions may change outcomes or their observation.
Statistical intervals, when enabled, are assumption-qualified; the recorded adjustment distinguishes pointwise and family confidence.
Sampled resource safeguards are not hard quotas or capacity guarantees.
No composite score, certification or production approval is defined.
Analytical bounds, retained-detail omissions, presentation omissions and privacy withholding are distinct. An unavailable metric is not zero.

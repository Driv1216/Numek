# CG4 implementation findings

Factual closure record, 2026-10-28. **Independent review and founder acceptance
have not occurred.** The [approved scope revision](CLOSURE_SCOPE_REVISION.md)
allows delivery without successive replacement-method research; it does not turn
failures or missing qualification into passes. Read [supported claims](SUPPORTED_METHODS.md)
and the [qualification matrix](QUALIFICATION_MATRIX.md) with this document.

This concise record groups significant issues. The complete earlier engineering
narrative, exact development boundaries and additional references are preserved in
[the historical findings](CG4_IMPLEMENTATION_FINDINGS_HISTORY.md). Its old future-work
statements are superseded by the approved revision. Logs are evidence, not pooled
independent experiments. Source/estimand-specific evidence is reused only where valid.

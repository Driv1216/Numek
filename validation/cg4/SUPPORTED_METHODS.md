# CG4 supported confirmation and discovery claims

Standing follows the [approved closure revision](CLOSURE_SCOPE_REVISION.md).

| Procedure | New formal confirmation | Assumptions / limits |
|---|---|---|
| Clopper–Pearson Bernoulli components and registered rate differences | Supported | IID eligible rows, frozen claim/denominators, complete allocated family; component Bonferroni permits dependent arms. |
| Registered confusion-cell F1 composition | Supported | Same IID population assumptions; simultaneous component intervals, not a direct normal approximation. |
| Fixed-bin calibration residual composition | Supported | Frozen bins, independent observations, bounded residual contributions; conservative intervals may be inconclusive. |
| Hoeffding bounded means and registered transforms | Supported | True bounds declared before H; violations refuse. No observed-extrema bound estimation. |
| Finite independent entity arms | Supported, narrow design | IID complete entities, at most one potential observation per entity in each frozen arm, before label filtering; cross-arm dependence allowed. No general unequal-cluster claim. |
| General BCa smooth mean/ratio and cluster bootstrap | Refused | Retained heavy-tail and unequal-cluster coverage failures. Research kernels and historical evidence remain readable. |
| General ranking percentile approximation | Refused | Existing 1,000-trial AUC/AP panels concern one frozen synthetic profile/family size; they do not justify general product confirmation. Native AUC/AP evaluation/discovery remains available. |
| Component BCa, bootstrap-t and known-variance Catoni candidates | Not registered | Research evidence retained; no successive replacement development for closure. |
| Temporal/common-shock inference and moving-block bootstrap | Refused | Dependence not supported; CG3 negative evidence retained. |
| Native nonadditive metrics without a registered interval, including R2 | Descriptive/exploratory | Native reducers remain authoritative. Pointwise proposal bounds do not establish native-metric intervals. |

All routes also require the frozen membership scope, outcome-observation estimand,
practical threshold, exposure provenance and study allocation. Missing provenance
does not prove independence. Formal support is not a causal/future guarantee.

Discovery remains available for its admitted objectives; availability is not a
recovery qualification claim. Current-selector qualification includes the completed
broad/narrow, interaction, parity, categorical/numeric and rare-failure profiles,
each 40/40 against the unchanged 32/40 target. These are five separate envelopes,
not universal recovery guarantees. Other retained standalone/old-selector results keep
their recorded scope; incomplete new panels remain unqualified. The landmark
algorithm failed promotion and remains experimental; the geometric fallback is
retained. Weak fidelity/stability, realistic text/image recovery and R2's
target-variance proxy limitation remain visible.

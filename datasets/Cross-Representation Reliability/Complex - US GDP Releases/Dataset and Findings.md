# US GDP Releases

US Bureau of Economic Analysis GDP tables and structured claims linked to selected numeric entries.

**Difficulty · Complex.** Two published documents and four recorded entries in the selected run, with typed cell and vintage checks. The label is based on the workload evaluated in Cross-Representation Reliability. [Publisher](https://www.bea.gov/news/2025/gross-domestic-product-4th-quarter-and-year-2024-third-estimate-gdp-industry-and).

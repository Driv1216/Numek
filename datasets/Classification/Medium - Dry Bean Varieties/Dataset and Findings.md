# Dry Bean Varieties

Shape and dimension measurements extracted from images of dry beans.

**Difficulty · Medium.** 13,611 rows, 16 measurement fields and seven labels. The label is based on the workload evaluated in Classification. [Publisher](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/602).

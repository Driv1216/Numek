# Linnerud Exercise Study

Exercise performance paired with weight, waist measurement and pulse.

**Difficulty · Simple.** 20 rows and three exercise inputs with three numerical outcomes. The label is based on the workload evaluated in Multi-Output Regression. [Publisher](https://scikit-learn.org/1.0/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_linnerud.html).

# COCO Captions and Image Labels

Text records made from COCO publisher captions and image identifiers.

**Difficulty · Complex.** 640 prepared records linking COCO image IDs and supplied captions. The label is based on the workload evaluated in Text Reliability. [Publisher](https://cocodataset.org/#download).

## Task and settings

Paired caption and generation records. The saved configuration declares these inputs and roles — Pairs (Corpus, Generation).

## What DataTrust found and what was checked

Independent checks verified joins and structural properties on the retained pairings. A second report covers the supplied multiple labels.

## Missed coverage and limitations

The audit does not establish semantic caption accuracy or generation quality.

## Usefulness

Useful for detecting pairing and structural problems.

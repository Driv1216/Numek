Bike Hour, from the repository’s saved regression validation report: 17,379 rows, 17 columns. The verified relationship is cnt = casual + registered. Both features are declared unavailable at prediction time.

The relationship has 100% finite-target coverage. That is evidence coverage, not a reliability score or a model-performance claim. Observed algebra alone does not prove leakage.

What every concept will preserve

NUMEK is the company. DataTrust is the implemented product. Classification scores retain their methodology; other families do not acquire an invented universal score.

Tabular, regression, comparative, temporal, text, image and cross-representation work are represented. ConfidenceGuard and HallucinationGuard remain future directions; audio is deferred.

Local execution, source evidence, explained methods, coverage limits and HTML/PDF/JSON reports remain central. Example collections are prototype content, not claims of persistent report history, accounts or hosted monitoring.

The implementation after review

Five independent concept layouts, each with a complete landing page, application home and results screen. Shared factual fixtures and navigation utilities only; no shared visual shell.

Desktop and mobile browser review, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, meaningful sample interactions, consistent screenshot sizes and a critical comparison.

All work stays in frontend-visual-lab-v4/. Production code and earlier experiments remain untouched. No commits, pushes or deployment.

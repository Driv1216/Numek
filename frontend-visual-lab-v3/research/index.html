Five ways NUMEK could look, each derived from what the research actually showed.

What we are designing. NUMEK is an AI reliability verification company; DataTrust is its one implemented product: local, evidence-first reliability analysis for tabular, regression, temporal, text, image and connected-document datasets, ending in HTML/PDF/JSON reports. ConfidenceGuard and HallucinationGuard are direction only and appear nowhere as products.

Shared content. Every concept uses the same real material: the Adult audit (48,842 × 15, final 69.0 Elevated after the critical-sub-score guardrail; evidence score 89.15) as the primary result, and the same run list — Bike hour regression (no score by design), SMS text, COCO subset, M9 finance records↔PDFs, plus German Credit and Bank Marketing classification runs. Differences you see are design, not invented features.

How the five differ

Research board

Frames are genuine headless-Chromium captures at 1440×900 (intro at 1 s, 3 s, 6 s, then every ~1,000 px of wheel scroll). Hover a card to play its screen recording. Status: live = original site inspected; gallery = only its Awwwards page could be inspected; partial = loader/gate or a stalled capture, not used as an influence.

Method and limits

  • 39 reference pages captured by research/scripts/capture-refs.mjs (Playwright, real wheel events so smooth-scroll libraries respond). 38 short recordings in research/motion/.
  • Key sites were also driven live in the in-app browser (LPAS, Pudding, Studio Freight, hellohello, State of AI Design).
  • Fonts and libraries per site come from the page itself (document.fonts, window globals) and are listed on each card.
  • Not inspected properly: Unseen 2025 (stuck at its loader), Siena (gated), GMB (scroll not advanced), Orchid and Mesh3D (capture hung on WebGL). ART+TECH, Praetorian and Elektra were seen only through Awwwards pages.
  • References are research only. No reference brand assets, logos or compositions are reused for NUMEK.

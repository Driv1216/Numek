Ten more ways NUMEK could look, drawn only from references not used in rounds one and two.

What was studied. 42 references: the library entries not yet used (Phantom, Unseen 2025, GMB, Immersive Garden, basement, Siena, Seasoned, ART+TECH, plus Vercel and Figma as secondary) and 32 individual Awwwards winners pulled from the Data Visualization, Experimental and Site of the Day/Month listings. Each was captured headlessly with gates opened, drags and hovers performed, ~12 scroll frames and a 45-second recording; every observation below is written from those captures.

Same content, same rules. All ten use the fixture of rounds one and two: the Adult audit (48,842 × 15; evidence 89.15 → final 69.0 Elevated, held by the critical-sub-score guardrail at target balance 47.86) and the same seven local runs. Only classification runs carry a score. No direction adds hosted monitoring, certification, collaboration or agent features, and ConfidenceGuard and HallucinationGuard remain research directions.

How the ten differ

Research board

Frames are genuine headless-Chromium captures at 1440×900 (in research/r4/captures). Hover a card to play its recording. Status: live = the original site was captured; gallery = only its Awwwards page; partial = a loader, gate or consent dialog blocked part of it; inaccessible = blocked or broken. Partial and inaccessible references inform nothing.

Method and limits

  • Winners were found by reading the Awwwards collection pages (research/scripts/awwwards-list.mjs) and each winner’s Awwwards page for its live URL and award (awwwards-sites.mjs). The Seventh Eye was excluded because round one already used it.
  • Captures by research/scripts/capture-deep.mjs: Siena’s Enter gate opened, Phantom’s wall dragged, GMB’s list hovered, long loaders waited out (Unseen 60 s+, Immersive Garden 16 s). Five were re-run (references-r4b.json).
  • Not inspected properly: Unseen 2025 (loader only — seen through its Awwwards page instead), Active Theory (“browser not supported”), US Avionix and Chladni (stalled at their gates), Subdivisions (geo-blocked 403), Navigate (SSL error), basement’s machine mode (timed out). F-List and Floema were seen only behind consent dialogs, which were neither accepted nor declined.
  • Headless capture uses software WebGL, so heavy 3D sites may move more smoothly in a real browser than the recordings suggest.
  • References are research only. No brand assets, logos or compositions are reused for NUMEK; each direction lists exactly what technique it borrows and what it leaves.

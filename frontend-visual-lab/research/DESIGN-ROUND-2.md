# Round two: design plan

**Brief, restated.** Round one turned each reference's subject matter into a costume: a film programme, hardware, bubbles. Round two learns only the craft from the references (how they present a product, their motion, type and finish) and applies it to what Numek actually is. Numek is an AI reliability verification company. DataTrust, its first product, gives an ML lead evidence about a dataset before training, plus a report a CTO or risk reviewer can trust. Every concept should be believable as Numek's real site and app.

**Shared content.** Real `datatrust audit` output for Adult and for the planted-leakage mutation: 12 checks, methods, column profiles, guardrails, the source SHA-256, a 0.20 s run, and the 5 conflicting groups (10 actual CSV lines).

**Review against the brief.** Each plan was checked against the generic default. Inspect's paper and orange would drift toward the cream and terracotta tell, so the paper is cooler, the orange is a true signal hue, and the identity comes from drawings and citations, not colour. Continual's near-black plus one accent risks the dark-mode tell, so the accent is used only on data lines, and the identity comes from the dithered sky and the line diagrams. Signal and Continual are both dark, so Signal is chromatic and product-dense while Continual is monochrome and diagrammatic. Assurance and Argument are both light, so Assurance is blue, object-led and gradient, while Argument is typographic and interactive.

Scope and limitations

Unavailable membership remains visible against the planned population. Conditional results cannot be read as full-population findings. Observed labels do not establish unobserved outcomes. Local overlap checks do not verify external independence; missing exposure history never proves freshness. Error control is study scoped, with no automatic guarantee across overlapping workspace studies.

Finite-sample procedures require their declared independence and observation assumptions. Under the founder-approved closure revision, inadequately qualified general BCa/cluster and ranking-percentile approximations refuse new formal confirmation; their research and historical evidence is retained. Temporal common shocks, arbitrary adaptive reuse, and CG3 moving-block bootstrap are not admitted CG4 confirmation routes. Historical restatements preserve original evidence and are descriptive unless an inferential schedule was frozen in advance.

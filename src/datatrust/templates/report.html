5. Methodology & Limitations

DataTrust V1 uses transparent checks for missingness, duplicates, class balance, feature usability, and heuristic leakage risk. Missing target labels are excluded from class calculations. Strong leakage candidates and severe findings can cap the overall score; every applied cap is shown above.

Review-only leakage prompts use conservative wording and do not automatically reduce the score. All leakage-name findings require confirmation of when the value becomes available.

The report is an evidence aid, not a certification.

Not included in V1: label-noise detection, drift monitoring, fairness testing, model calibration, human review of business rules, or regulatory compliance determination.

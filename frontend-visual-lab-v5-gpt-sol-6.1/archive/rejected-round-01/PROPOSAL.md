Research and static composition studies only. Implementation awaits the review requested in the brief.

Open `research/index.html` for the visual board, actual browser captures, five three-screen studies and inspection limits.

## 1. Open the finding

The product starts with something worth investigating.

- Structure: Asymmetric finding stage; horizontal application navigation; three reading lanes in results.
- Typeface candidates: Archivo Semi Condensed + Instrument Sans.
- Palette: #F9FBFD, #14222F, #214CD8, #E54A2B, #E6EEF8.
- Motion: A single finding expands into its evidence lanes; selection highlights only the corresponding population. Reduced motion uses the same states without travel.
- Actual influences: AI in Design: bold-to-dense reading rhythm. Obys: typographic hierarchy. Linear: coherent selected-item detail.
- Design risk: The headline and evidence stage must be composed together; otherwise this could regress into a conventional software hero.

## 2. A report worth reading

The report itself becomes the company’s most persuasive object.

- Structure: Offset report leaf on a plum field; a session reading desk; chapter navigation and contextual evidence margins.
- Typeface candidates: Newsreader + Manrope.
- Palette: #352240, #F7F5FF, #EDEA93, #C1B2CA, #ECE8F0.
- Motion: The report changes scale and settles into a flat reading plane; margin annotations appear on selection. No page-curl imitation or motion in every panel.
- Actual influences: Micro: continuity between distinct material states. Siena: strong framing around the subject. AI in Design: navigable report hierarchy.
- Design risk: The serif voice must still feel like technical software; dense evidence should use the sans face and remain quick to scan.

## 3. Follow the evidence

Make the connection between an observation and its source visible.

- Structure: A composed source-to-finding-to-document panorama; relationship selection; exact source inspector.
- Typeface candidates: Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk.
- Palette: #EEEAFB, #2A1740, #6644D8, #FCB3A0, #FAF9FD.
- Motion: Focus follows a selected connection, preserving source identity and context as the composition unfolds. The application uses direct highlight and panel resizing.
- Actual influences: Hamilton: one selection drives narrative and evidence. Hyperbolic: a technical thesis becomes a coherent graphic system. LPAS: a sustained spatial composition.
- Design risk: The relationship graphic must stay bounded and legible. It must never become a free-floating network or imply general claim verification.

## 4. Put it side by side

Analytical precision expressed through aligned comparison.

- Structure: A split landing composition; full-width source ledger; paired, aligned evidence panes.
- Typeface candidates: Space Grotesk + Source Sans 3.
- Palette: #124D50, #D7EDE7, #F5F8F5, #E86D41, #10373A.
- Motion: The dividing seam moves only to reveal a corresponding comparison. Shared axes and source identifiers stay fixed; keyboard controls provide the same states.
- Actual influences: LPAS: adjacent panels retain context. Hamilton: dim unrelated evidence rather than redrawing everything. ART+TECH: strong identities can contain disciplined statistical content.
- Design risk: A seam or slider can become a gimmick. All essential values must remain visible and the layout must stack cleanly on narrow screens.

## 5. Bring the whole picture into view

Give evidence, context and analytical coverage equal visual weight.

- Structure: A large composed evidence surface; compact navigation rail; a coverage-oriented analysis workspace with focused detail sheets.
- Typeface candidates: Sora + Public Sans.
- Palette: #2923C7, #201842, #DAD6F5, #F2B5BD, #F8FAFD.
- Motion: Evidence groups reflow between overview and selected detail while retaining their identity. Application transitions are short and user-triggered.
- Actual influences: Cash App: UI primitives carry brand across scales. ART+TECH: statistical forms belong to the identity. Phantom: an overview can lead into focused content.
- Design risk: This is closest to familiar product UI. Its composition, type scale and evidence grouping need the strongest iteration to avoid a generic card dashboard.

## Critical comparison

Open the finding gives the clearest direct product demonstration. A report worth reading has the strongest report-sharing identity but must preserve technical speed. Follow the evidence has the most specific connection to M9 and source inspection but must also handle ordinary tabular analysis well. Put it side by side is strongest for investigation and comparisons but needs a careful narrow-screen fallback. Bring the whole picture into view offers the broadest product system and is most at risk of looking conventional without composition refinement.

These are hypotheses illustrated by composition studies, not claims of achieved launch quality. All five still need complete landing pages, runnable interfaces, actual motion, responsive refinement and browser testing after review.

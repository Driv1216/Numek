Five directions

Shared product truth; different composition, typography, application structure and motion. Each would become a complete landing page, a usable application home and an analytical results screen after review.

Direction 1

Open the finding

The product starts with something worth investigating.

One identity, three experiences

Landing. An enlarged, inspectable finding is the opening visual. Scrolling opens its evidence, resolves the interpretation, then reveals modalities, methodology, local execution and exports.

Home. A horizontal session workbench with clear dataset rows and one selected analysis. No service history or hosted monitoring.

Results. Finding, source evidence and interpretation stay visible in adjacent lanes. The reader can open group details without losing the question.

Craft and source

Type candidates. Archivo Semi Condensed + Instrument Sans. Studies use local system faces as visual stand-ins.

Motion. A single finding expands into its evidence lanes; selection highlights only the corresponding population. Reduced motion uses the same states without travel.

Influences. AI in Design: bold-to-dense reading rhythm. Obys: typographic hierarchy. Linear: coherent selected-item detail.

Design risk. The headline and evidence stage must be composed together; otherwise this could regress into a conventional software hero.

Direction 2

A report worth reading

The report itself becomes the company’s most persuasive object.

One identity, three experiences

Landing. A composed report opens into a continuous reading sequence: overview, finding, distribution, methods, coverage and local exports. Product evidence occupies the large visual surfaces.

Home. Opened sample reports sit on a quiet reading desk with clear titles and task types. The desk represents the active local session.

Results. A readable report with quiet chapter navigation, a technical evidence margin and an export toolbar. Detailed inspection remains available.

Craft and source

Type candidates. Newsreader + Manrope. Studies use local system faces as visual stand-ins.

Motion. The report changes scale and settles into a flat reading plane; margin annotations appear on selection. No page-curl imitation or motion in every panel.

Influences. Micro: continuity between distinct material states. Siena: strong framing around the subject. AI in Design: navigable report hierarchy.

Design risk. The serif voice must still feel like technical software; dense evidence should use the sans face and remain quick to scan.

Direction 3

Follow the evidence

Make the connection between an observation and its source visible.

One identity, three experiences

Landing. One real kind of DataTrust relationship carries the story: record → declared comparison → document context. Later sections show tabular, temporal, text and image evidence, private inspection and reports.

Home. Sources and relationships take priority over metric cards. Sample cases include the same Concrete regression review and an explicitly synthetic connected-evidence bundle.

Results. Source values and native document context sit beside the finding. Exact discrepancies, model candidates and unresolved evidence receive different treatments.

Craft and source

Type candidates. Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk. Studies use local system faces as visual stand-ins.

Motion. Focus follows a selected connection, preserving source identity and context as the composition unfolds. The application uses direct highlight and panel resizing.

Influences. Hamilton: one selection drives narrative and evidence. Hyperbolic: a technical thesis becomes a coherent graphic system. LPAS: a sustained spatial composition.

Design risk. The relationship graphic must stay bounded and legible. It must never become a free-floating network or imply general claim verification.

Direction 4

Put it side by side

Analytical precision expressed through aligned comparison.

One identity, three experiences

Landing. Two observations from the same feature-identical group establish the need for investigation. The story widens to repetition, dataset comparisons, timing and reports without calling every difference an error.

Home. A source ledger organizes datasets and groups. One selection opens a paired comparison with shared labels and clear population boundaries.

Results. Paired source evidence stays aligned. The Concrete example compares the lower and upper target within one observed group, while explaining legitimate causes.

Craft and source

Type candidates. Space Grotesk + Source Sans 3. Studies use local system faces as visual stand-ins.

Motion. The dividing seam moves only to reveal a corresponding comparison. Shared axes and source identifiers stay fixed; keyboard controls provide the same states.

Influences. LPAS: adjacent panels retain context. Hamilton: dim unrelated evidence rather than redrawing everything. ART+TECH: strong identities can contain disciplined statistical content.

Design risk. A seam or slider can become a gimmick. All essential values must remain visible and the layout must stack cleanly on narrow screens.

Direction 5

Bring the whole picture into view

Give evidence, context and analytical coverage equal visual weight.

One identity, three experiences

Landing. Evidence groups form a recognizable brand language. The selected dataset supplies meaningful numbers and a genuine distribution; later sections disclose modality scope, methods, local evidence and shareable reports.

Home. The selected dataset anchors a coverage navigator. Computed diagnostics, bounded searches and unavailable evidence are distinguishable without an invented overall score.

Results. A full-width analytical canvas groups related evidence; selecting a finding opens a detail sheet with source references and methods.

Craft and source

Type candidates. Sora + Public Sans. Studies use local system faces as visual stand-ins.

Motion. Evidence groups reflow between overview and selected detail while retaining their identity. Application transitions are short and user-triggered.

Influences. Cash App: UI primitives carry brand across scales. ART+TECH: statistical forms belong to the identity. Phantom: an overview can lead into focused content.

Design risk. This is closest to familiar product UI. Its composition, type scale and evidence grouping need the strongest iteration to avoid a generic card dashboard.

Product truth in every direction

NUMEK is the company. DataTrust is the implemented product. ConfidenceGuard and HallucinationGuard remain future directions. The prototypes will represent local execution, inspectable evidence and HTML/PDF/JSON reports.

Classification scores belong to their supported policy. No universal regression, temporal, text, image or connected-evidence score will be invented. Facts, candidates, coverage limits and unresolved context remain distinct.

The report-desk and session examples do not imply persistent history, accounts, cloud monitoring, certification or enterprise collaboration.

Evidence and next step

The shared Concrete values come from the existing completed regression report: 1,030 rows; 9 columns; 25 repeated rows; 11 exact groups; 36 participating rows; 9 feature-identical groups with varying targets and 20 participating rows. Its 19-bin target histogram is reproduced from that report.

The connected document example is explicitly synthetic. It illustrates an implemented interaction family without implying a customer case or general semantic guarantee.

After direction review: five independent implementations, full landing narratives, functional navigation, responsive iteration, consistent browser screenshots, and proportional verification. No production integration, commits or pushes.
